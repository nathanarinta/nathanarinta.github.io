Support

Something not right? Here's how to fix the common stuff, or reach a real person.

Contact us

Email us and we'll usually reply within 2 business days. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened right before the problem.

Email support

I bought ad-free or gems and don't see them

Open the Store tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still doesn't show up, email us and include the order ID from your Apple receipt email.

I got a new phone

Ad-free carries over: install Driftline and tap Restore purchases in the Store tab. Coins, gems you've already spent, upgrades and cars are saved on your old device only, so they don't transfer.

I deleted the app and lost my progress

Game progress lives on your device, so deleting the app removes it and we can't recover it. Ad-free can still be restored.

I'd like a refund

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I turn off personalized ones?

Ads keep Driftline free. To stop personalized ads, deny tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. To remove ads that play on their own, buy ad-free in the Store tab. More detail is in our privacy policy.

The game is laggy or crashing

Close other apps, make sure iOS is up to date, and restart your phone. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and we'll look into it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data on your device. To remove the anonymous purchase record we keep for restoring purchases, email us and we'll delete it.
