Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • You don't make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your coins, upgrades and cars are saved on your phone, not on our servers.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Purchases are handled by Apple and verified through RevenueCat. Those services collect some device data, described below.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

Driftline ("the app") is made by Arinta Engineering LLC, based in Kenosha, Wisconsin, USA ("we", "us"). You can reach us at driftline.support@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, including coins, gems, upgrades, unlocked cars, missions and settings, is stored locally on your device. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Driftline shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is only shared if you allow tracking when the app asks. If you decline, ads are not personalized using it.
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language.
  • How you interact with ads, and basic diagnostic and performance data.

Google's use of this data is covered by how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from the app's settings.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases, such as removing ads or buying gems, are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, your purchase and transaction history, and basic device information such as model, operating system version and App Store country. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Crash and diagnostic data

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use these only to fix bugs.

How we use information

  • To show ads in the free version of the game and keep it free to play.
  • To deliver, verify and restore your purchases.
  • To fix crashes and improve the game.
  • To reply when you contact us for support.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above so the app can work, or when required by law.

Advertising through AdMob may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some US state privacy laws. You can stop it by declining tracking when asked, or anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Your choices

  • Tracking: allow or deny it when prompted, or change it later in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Ads: buying ad-free removes ads that play on their own.
  • Local data: delete the app to remove all game data stored on your device.
  • Purchase records: email us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous ID. Your purchase history with Apple is managed by Apple.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, UK, California and other US states, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it's used. Because we don't collect your name or contact details in the app, the simplest way to use these rights is to email us. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Driftline is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Data retention and security

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to restore your purchases and meet legal requirements. Data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
